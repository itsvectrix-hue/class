Privacy policy

This page explains what happens when you browse the site or use the lesson enquiry form.

Updated 13 November 2026

Information in the enquiry form

The form asks for your name, phone number, lesson interest and an optional message. The site uses those details in your browser to prepare a WhatsApp message addressed to +91 90286 71906.

The form does not submit those details to a database or send them to the website. WhatsApp opens with the message ready. You decide whether to send it.

When you contact the class

When you send a WhatsApp message or make a phone call, the information you share is handled through that service and by the person you contact. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to its service.

Read WhatsApp's privacy policy .

Website hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like other hosting providers, Vercel may process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. Its privacy policy explains how that service handles information.

Read Vercel's privacy policy .

Your choices

You can browse the website without filling in the enquiry form. Do not include information in a message that you do not want to share with the class or the messaging service.

Questions

For a question about information you have shared directly with the class, call +91 90286 71906 or send a WhatsApp message.
