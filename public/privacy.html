Privacy policy

This page explains what happens when you browse the Tonexa Music Academy website or prepare a lesson enquiry.

Updated 18 November 2026

Information in the enquiry form

The form can include your name, an optional phone number, instrument interest, area or locality, and an optional message. Submitting the form prepares an email draft addressed to tonexa.music.acadmy@gmail.com using your device's email application.

The website does not send the form details to a database. Your email application opens with the draft for you to review. The message is sent only if you choose to send it.

When you contact the academy

When you send an email, the information you choose to share is handled by your email provider and by Tonexa Music Academy. Your email provider's privacy policy applies to its service.

Website hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel. The hosting service may process technical request information to deliver and protect the site.

Read Vercel's privacy policy .

Your choices

You can browse the website without filling in the enquiry form. Only include information in an email that you are comfortable sharing with the academy and your email provider.

Questions

For a question about information you have shared directly with the academy, email tonexa.music.acadmy@gmail.com.
